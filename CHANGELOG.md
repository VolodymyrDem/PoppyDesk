# Changelog

## 0.5.0

- **Trackpad NumPad**: lay out your own keys. Choose **My Own** in its settings and **Edit Keys…**: drag keys onto the 4×5 grid and make Space, Shift, Enter or any other key as wide or tall as you like. Add letters, keystrokes like ⌘C, apps, files, links and shortcuts from the Shortcuts app. Apps, files and shortcuts show their own icon.
- **Trackpad NumPad**: keys are rounded like Apple's app icons. Make more than one page and swipe sideways with one finger to turn them.
- **Trackpad NumPad**: modifiers hold on for the next key you tap, and Caps Lock stays on until you tap it again.
- **Trackpad NumPad**: choose which corner of the trackpad opens it with a double tap.
- **Trackpad NumPad**: the arrow keys are easier to hit, and the keys keep their size when the notch is wider.

## 0.4.0

- **Trackpad NumPad** (beta): press ⌥⌘N, or double-tap the trackpad's top-left corner, and the trackpad's right half becomes a numeric keypad that comes out of the notch. Tap or click a key to type it; Esc puts it away.
- **Quick Wheel**: double-tap anywhere on the trackpad with two fingers to open it under the pointer. It takes the place of Smart Zoom; turn it off in its settings.
- **Quick Wheel**: swipe sideways with two fingers to turn the page.
- **Notch**: the closed notch matches the camera notch exactly, down to the half point, with rounder corners like the real one.

## 0.3.0

- **Quick Wheel** (beta): hold ⌃⌥⌘ for a wheel of brightness, volume, media, Mission Control, PoppyDesk's own things and your shortcuts, apps, links and keystrokes. Let go over one, or tap the keys and click.
- **Notch**: what's playing steps aside while an app is full screen; turn that off in **Settings → Notch**.
- **Clipboard Wheel**: turn the pages with ← → too, like the Quick Wheel. A held arrow key turns them one after another without flickering.
- **Quick Wheel**: your shortcuts show their own icon from the Shortcuts app.
- **Quick Wheel**: the poppy sits in the middle and turns into a cross while you rest on it. Swap it for your own picture, as an icon or filling the circle, in its settings.
- **Sound**: an app turned below 100% no longer goes silent. Calls show as FaceTime instead of a background service.
- **Onboarding and Settings** show PoppyDesk's own module icons.

## 0.2.2

- **Notch**: the closed notch is now exactly the size of the camera notch, like the real one. Make it bigger in **Settings → Notch** if you'd like.
- **Notch**: the camera on Home is a card like the others until you click it, instead of a black block.
- **Settings**: sliders are wider, with − and + for small steps and a button to reset them.

## 0.2.1

- **Notch**: the open notch's bottom corners now follow the cards inside them, so the black edge around them is the same width all the way round.

## 0.2.0

- **Keep Awake** is its own module now: put its cup in the notch or in the menu bar, where it counts down beside the icon.
- **Keep Awake** has 30 minutes instead of 5 hours, and **Custom…** for any length or until a time of day. Type it like the notch timer, or scroll over the hours and minutes, with a tap on the trackpad for each step.
- **Menu Bar** is in beta: it's off until you turn it on, and is turned off once after this update. Turn it back on in **Settings → Modules** if you'd like it.

## 0.1.0

The first release. Turn on only the modules you want in **Settings → Modules**.

- **Notch**: hover it for what's playing, your week, timers and a stopwatch, a shelf for files and AirDrop, and the camera as a mirror. Black, glass or clear glass.
- **Clipboard Wheel**: drag files, tap Control, and drop them on Copy, Cut, Move To, AirDrop, Quick Look and more.
- **Clipboard History**: everything you copy, a shortcut away, with search and pins, also in the notch.
- **Menu Bar**: click the poppy to hide the icons left of its divider.
- **Battery**: charge, health and where the power goes, with the watts coming in shown by the notch.
- **Keep Awake**: keeps your Mac up for a while, with a countdown beside the notch.
- **Eye Breaks**: reminds you to look at something far away now and then.
- **Sound**: each app's volume, and where the sound goes.
