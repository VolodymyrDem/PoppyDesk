Your Mac already has a notch, a trackpad and a menu bar. PoppyDesk gives each of them more to do.

Ten modules, all optional

Switch on the ones you want in Settings. One that's off doesn't run and never asks for a permission.

Notch

Hover the notch and it opens into a dashboard: what's playing, your week, a timer and a stopwatch, a shelf for files and AirDrop.

Clipboard Wheel

Drag files or anything else, tap Control, and drop on Copy, Cut, Copy Path, AirDrop and more.

Quick Wheel Beta

Hold ⌃⌥⌘ or double tap the trackpad with two fingers for a wheel of brightness, volume, media, Mission Control and your own shortcuts, apps and links. Swipe to turn the page.

NumPad Beta

Press ⌥⌘N and the trackpad's right half becomes a numeric keypad, shown coming out of the notch. Tap or click a key to type it, or lay out keys of your own.

Clipboard History

Everything you copy, a shortcut away, with search and pins, pasted right where you were.

Menu Bar Beta

Click the poppy to hide the icons left of its divider, and again to bring them back. Off until you turn it on.

Battery

How full it is and where the power goes, with a bubble by the notch showing the watts coming in.

Keep Awake

Keeps the Mac from sleeping for as long as you like, with a little cup counting down by the notch or in the menu bar.

Eye Breaks

Every so often, a blinking eye reminds you to look at something far away for a few seconds.

Sound

Turn each app up, down or off, and pick where the sound goes, from the menu bar or the notch.

How to install

PoppyDesk isn't from the App Store yet, so a downloaded copy needs your OK once. Paste one line into Terminal and it's skipped.

Fastest: in Terminal
curl -fsSL https://volodymyrdem.github.io/PoppyDesk/install.sh | bash

Downloads the latest PoppyDesk from GitHub, puts it in Applications and opens it. See what it does.

Or with the download

Download

Open PoppyDesk.dmg and drag PoppyDesk into Applications.

Open it

Open PoppyDesk from Applications. macOS says it can't check it; click Done.

Allow it

Scroll to the bottom of Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway next to PoppyDesk.

Open Settings

Enjoy

Pick your modules. From then on PoppyDesk updates itself, no more asking.

Questions

Is PoppyDesk free?

Yes, every module. There's nothing to buy and no account to make.

Why does macOS warn about it?

Apps from outside the App Store need to be checked by Apple to open without a question. PoppyDesk isn't yet, so macOS asks you to confirm once, unless you install it from Terminal. Updates install without asking again.

How does it update?

PoppyDesk checks for a new version once a day and offers to install it in a click. Turn that off, or have updates install on their own, in Settings → About.

Does it work on Macs without a notch?

Yes. Macs without one get a black notch at the top of the screen that works the same way.

Which permissions does it ask for?

Only what the modules you turn on need, the first time you use them: Accessibility to paste, to tidy the menu bar and to open the Quick Wheel from any app, Calendar to show your events, the camera for the mirror in the notch, audio for the Sound mixer, and Finder to move files you cut. Each is explained before macOS asks.

How do I uninstall it?

Quit it from the poppy in the menu bar and drag PoppyDesk from Applications to the Trash.

Found a bug or have an idea?

Tell me on GitHub, or use Settings → About → Report a Problem in the app, which fills in your versions for you.
